About NaumiCrowns

Premium Hair Atelier — Every crown tells a story.

Our Story

NaumiCrowns is a South African wig atelier founded and run by co-owners George and Naumi. Every wig we sell is made by our own hands in our workshop — we don't resell mass-produced stock, we craft each crown, inspect it, and only then does it earn the NaumiCrowns name.

We started with a simple belief: premium-quality wigs shouldn't cost premium-boutique prices. Our range runs from R350 for an 8-inch Bob to R750 for a 30-inch Bouncy Human Blend — high quality, low prices, no compromise.

What We Make

Bob wigs (8"–14", R350–R650) — the everyday classic, sleek and effortless. Bouncy Human Blend (26"–30", R680–R750) — soft curls with natural volume and movement. Human Hair Blend (28"–30", R680–R700) — silky, full and natural-looking, our best-value range.

Delivery & Our Promise

We deliver in Johannesburg and Polokwane, and nationwide across South Africa via Paxi (PEP Stores, R60) and PostNet (R100). Delivery takes 2–6 business days — and if we're late, you get R50 off your next crown. Orders over R2,500 ship free.

How Ordering Works

Browse the collection, add your crown to the bag, and check out — payment is 100% upfront via PayShap or Capitec cellphone payment. You can track your order live on our tracking page from the moment it's received to the moment it's delivered.
